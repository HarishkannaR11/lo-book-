## English (en-IN)

**App name**
```
Lorry Book - லாரி கணக்கு
```

**Short description**
```
Trip, diesel and dues book for lorry owners. Works even without signal.
```

**Full description**
```
A simple book for small lorry owners. No notebook, no calculator.

• Trips: vehicle, party, from, to, freight — entered in a minute.
• Diesel, toll, bata and repair expenses. Photograph the bunk slip and litres and amount fill in by themselves.
• Profit and mileage the moment a trip is closed.
• Dues per party, with part payments counted.
• Send a dues reminder on WhatsApp in one tap.
• A red flag when a lorry's mileage drops below its own usual.
• Warnings before FC, insurance, permit or PUC expire.
• Monthly report: trips, freight, expenses and profit per vehicle.
• Works without signal; entries are sent when the signal returns.
• Tamil, English, Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, Punjabi.

Your accounts are visible only to you. No ads.
```
