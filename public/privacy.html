Privacy policy

Lorry Book (லாரி கணக்கு) · Last updated 13 November 2026

Lorry Book is a trip, diesel and dues book for small lorry owners. This page explains what the app stores, why, and how you can delete it. The same app runs on the web at lo-book.vercel.app and as the Android app on Google Play.

What we store

  • Your mobile number, to sign you in with a one-time password (OTP).
  • Your name and business name, and the language you choose.
  • What you enter: vehicles and their documents' expiry dates; drivers' names and mobile numbers; parties' names, places and mobile numbers; trips, odometer readings, diesel and other expenses; payments received.
  • Photos you choose to save with an entry: diesel bunk slips and vehicle documents (FC, insurance, permit, PUC).

We do not collect your location, contacts, call logs or any data from other apps. There are no ads and no advertising or analytics trackers.

How it is used

  • Only to run the app for you: to show your trips, profit, mileage, dues and reminders.
  • Reading a slip or document photo (OCR) happens on your phone. The photo is uploaded only if you save it with the entry.
  • Until your phone has signal, new entries are kept on your phone and sent when it reconnects. Signing out removes them from the phone.
  • The WhatsApp reminder only opens WhatsApp with a message for you to send. The app sends nothing by itself.
  • If you add a driver's mobile number, that driver can sign in and add expenses to their own open trip. Drivers cannot see freight, payments, profit or any other owner's data.

Who else processes it

We do not sell or share your data. These service providers process it only to run the app:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and photo storage.
  • Vercel: hosts the app.
  • The SMS provider used by Supabase receives your mobile number to deliver the OTP.

Security

All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules keep each owner's data visible only to that owner. Photos are private and are shown only through short-lived links.

Delete your account

  • In the app: open Settings, tap Delete account, then Yes, delete everything.
  • Without the app: go to lo-book.vercel.app/settings, sign in with your mobile number, and do the same.

This immediately and permanently deletes your account and everything in it: vehicles, drivers, parties, trips, expenses, payments, documents and photos. Nothing is kept, except that copies may remain in our providers' routine backups until those backups expire.

Children

The app is for business owners and is not meant for anyone under 18.

Contact and changes

For any question or request about your data, write to the developer email address shown on the app's Google Play page. If this policy changes, the date above will change.
